If you distribute, host, or commercialize a modified version of the software, you must rebrand it unless you have explicit written permission from the Mindris maintainers.

You may not, without prior written permission:

- use `Mindris` or `Mindris AI` as the primary name of a fork, product, or hosted service

- imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or sponsorship by the Mindris project

Examples of prohibited uses without approval:
